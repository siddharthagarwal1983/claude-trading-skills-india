# India Stock Research Note Template

```markdown
# <Company> (<NSE symbol>) — Research Note
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD | **Price:** ₹x,xxx.xx (as of <session>) | **Tier:** Large/Mid/Small | **Industry:** <NSE industry>

## 1. Summary
Three to five sentences: what the business is, what changed recently, the main debate.

## 2. Business
Segments and revenue mix, market position, key customers or products, promoter group.

## 3. Results
| Period | Revenue ₹ cr | YoY | EBITDA margin | Net profit ₹ cr | YoY | EPS ₹ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
Latest quarters and the last two fiscal years (April-March). Note one-offs and exceptional items.

## 4. Balance Sheet and Cash Flow
Debt, net cash or net debt, working capital, operating cash flow vs profit, capex.
(Quarterly filings carry no cash flow; use annual figures.)

## 5. Valuation
P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA now vs the stock's own history and peers; dividend yield.

## 6. Ownership
Promoter %, FII %, DII/MF % and quarterly changes; promoter pledge %; notable bulk/block deals.

## 7. Technical Picture
Trend vs 50/200-day averages, 52-week position, relative strength vs NIFTY 500, RSI, ATR %,
turnover, next results date (from the fact sheet).

## 8. Bull Case / Bear Case
Three points each, each tied to evidence.

## 9. Key Risks
From the India risk checklist; only those that apply.

## 10. What to Watch
Specific numbers and dates (next results, guidance items, pledge changes, price levels).

## Sources
NSE data (fact sheet), DalalOS company report sections used, news links with dates.
```

## Style

- Indian units: ₹ lakh and ₹ crore (1 crore = 10 million); fiscal years as FY2026 (April
  2025-March 2026); quarters as Q2 FY27.
- State the period and source for every figure. Distinguish consolidated from standalone.
- No target prices or buy/sell recommendations; describe scenarios and evidence.
