# India-Specific Risk Checklist

Check each item against the company report, filings and news; include only those that apply.

## Governance and ownership
- **Promoter pledge:** pledged share of the promoter holding, and its trend. High or rising
  pledge raises forced-selling risk in a falling market.
- **Promoter selling** or creeping stake changes; large bulk/block deals by promoters.
- **Related-party transactions:** large purchases from, sales to, loans to or guarantees for
  promoter entities, relative to revenue or net worth.
- **Auditor:** resignation, qualified opinion or emphasis of matter; frequent auditor changes.
- **Group exposure:** debt or cash flows tied to stressed group companies.

## Market structure
- **Surveillance lists (ASM/GSM):** stocks under additional surveillance need higher margins
  and may move to trade-for-trade settlement, cutting liquidity.
- **Price bands:** 2%, 5% or 10% bands limit exits on bad news; note the band.
- **F&O ban:** for F&O stocks, the ban list (open interest above 95% of the limit) blocks new
  derivative positions and often coincides with volatile price action.
- **Low free float / thin turnover:** large positions relative to average daily turnover are
  hard to exit.
- **Upcoming events:** results date, record dates, OFS by promoters or the government,
  index inclusion or exclusion.

## Business and regulation
- **PSU status:** government policy can override shareholder returns (pricing, dividends,
  divestment, social obligations).
- **Regulated sectors:** RBI (banks, NBFCs), IRDAI (insurers), SEBI (market intermediaries),
  TRAI (telecom), CERC/SERC (power), drug regulators and USFDA observations (pharma).
- **Commodity and currency exposure:** metals, oil and chemicals follow global prices; IT and
  pharma exporters are exposed to the rupee and US demand.
- **Working capital:** rising receivables or inventory faster than revenue; cash flow well
  below reported profit.
- **Leverage:** debt/EBITDA, interest coverage, refinancing needs; for financials, asset
  quality (GNPA/NNPA) and capital adequacy.

## Corporate actions and data
- **Demergers and restructurings:** price history before the ex-date is not comparable; the
  fact sheet flags unexplained jumps.
- **Bonus/split history:** compare per-share figures only after restating.
